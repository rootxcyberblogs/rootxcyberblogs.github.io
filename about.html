About

Angad Singh

I'm a Cybersecurity Specialist currently leading the security function at Centilytics, where I oversee the company's security posture and run penetration testing engagements across infrastructure and web applications — for both internal systems and client-facing platforms.

My work sits at the intersection of offensive and defensive security. I spend most of my time finding what breaks before someone else does — whether that's an authentication bypass in a banking portal, an IDOR in a GraphQL API, or a misconfigured switch leaving a network open to MITM attacks.

This blog is where I document what I learn. It's technical, hands-on, and written for people who want to understand the why, not just the steps.
